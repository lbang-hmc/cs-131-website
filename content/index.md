## Class Meeting Times

In-class practical learning sessions (times to work specifically on course assignments and labs):

- **Tuesdays & Thursdays, 9:35am – 10:50am Pacific Time**, in MCSC 203 / 204

**Course format:** This is a flipped class. You'll spend time outside of class focusing on theoretical learning (by engaging with online content) and time in class focusing on synthesizing theoretical and practical learning by working on assignments and problems on your own, with a partner, or in small groups.
