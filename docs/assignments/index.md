# Assignments

An overview of assignments will be posted here as they're published. To start:

- [**HW 1: peoPLe**](hw01.md) — get oriented to the syllabus, the history and culture of programming languages, and how this course works.
- [**HW 2: FUNctional programming**](hw02.md) — recursion, pattern matching, and higher-order functions, ending with building arithmetic itself out of a single function.
- [**HW 3: Lists, Datatypes, and a Stack Machine**](hw03.md) — more list and higher-order-function practice, your own datatype, and evaluating arithmetic expressions with a stack machine.
- [**HW 4: Little Languages (Random Art and Regular Expressions)**](hw04.md) — generate random art from expression trees, and implement lazy regular-expression matching.
